# 行为准则 / Code of Conduct

> 中文在前，英文在后。Chinese first, English below.

## 中文

参与本项目即表示你同意：

- 尊重他人，不进行人身攻击、骚扰、歧视或辱骂。
- 不在 Issue、PR 或讨论区发布赌博、成人、诈骗、引流广告等内容——本项目本身就会过滤这类情报。
- 对事不对人：不同意方案时说理由，不说狠话。
- 维护者有权删除违规内容、关闭违规讨论，严重时封禁账号。

## English

By participating in this project you agree to:

- Treat others with respect. No personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, or abuse.
- Do not post gambling, adult, scam, or spam content in issues, PRs, or discussions — this project filters out exactly that kind of content.
- Criticize ideas, not people: disagree with reasons, not insults.
- Maintainers may remove violating content, close violating discussions, and ban accounts in serious cases.
